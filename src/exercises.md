# Exercises (Workbook)

Jay's rule: **"learning is behavior change — if you don't change your behavior, did you actually learn?"**

Complete these in order. Each maps to a chapter.

## 1. Foundations (ch. 1)
- [ ] Compute your LTV and CAC for one offer; note the ratio.
- [ ] Identify which demand type (current or future) you're under-investing in, and pick one action for this week.

## 2. Six P's (ch. 2)
- [ ] Fill in all six P's for your offer: People / Positioning / Promise / Proof / Priority / Process.
- [ ] Run the "so what?" test on your promise until you reach the core outcome.
- [ ] Pass the proof through the deaf-and-mute test (can you *point* at it?).

## 3. Empathy map (ch. 3)
- [ ] Draw the empathy map: current reality / fears / goals.
- [ ] Mine 10 verbatim customer phrases (sales calls, tickets, comments).
- [ ] Convert 3 phrases into hooks.

## 4. Personal brand (ch. 4)
- [ ] Write your PTL demonstration plan (power, trust, likeness — verbal + visual).
- [ ] Define your transformation niche (A → Z) and list 5 subtopics.
- [ ] Schedule one PTL-demonstrating post this week.

## 5. Content multiplication (ch. 5)
- [ ] Build your Hall of Fame: 5 best posts with metrics.
- [ ] For the top post: 1 repost (90 days), 2 repurposes, 3 remixes.

## 6. Ad writing (ch. 6)
- [ ] 1-page Story Bible for a real offer.
- [ ] 5-line why-they-buy sheet.
- [ ] Write one kaleidoscoped ad: 5 hooks, 1 body, 1 CTA; run the 5-point hook checklist before shipping.

## 7. Media buying (ch. 7)
- [ ] Reframe your ad objective from CPL to AOV/LTGP.
- [ ] Plan a 15–30 creative batch per $10K of spend.
- [ ] Audit campaign rebuilds against Meta's 50-conversion / 14-day learning window.

## The through-line, again

> "The game is won before the pen is even used." Research first, writing second.
