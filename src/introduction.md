# Introduction

**Course:** *Writing Words That Make People Buy* — a study course distilled from Jay Yang's three "Full Courses" (his "trilogy"):

1. *How To Write Words That Make People Buy* (43.5 min)
2. *How to Build a Personal Brand That People Buy From* (22.9 min)
3. *How to Create Ads That Make People Buy* (37.2 min)

## How to use this course

Jay's rule: **"learning is behavior change"** — "if you watch a video and you don't change your behavior, then did you actually learn?" Each chapter ends with a short exercise from the [workbook](exercises.md). Do the exercise before moving on.

## What you'll be able to do

- Think about copy before writing it — the **six P's** (chapter 2)
- Build an **empathy map** and mine sales calls for the exact words your buyers use (chapter 3)
- Build a brand that creates **future demand** using **PTL** (chapter 4)
- Become prolific with the **content multiplication system**: Hall of Fame + the three R's (chapter 5)
- Run the **5-step ad writing process** for any client or yourself (chapter 6)
- Buy media like Jay's team: optimize for **AOV/LTGP**, run 15–30 creatives per $10K (chapter 7)

## The through-line

> "The game is won before the pen is even used." — research first, writing second.
