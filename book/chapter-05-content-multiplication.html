Writing Words That Make People Buy

Content Multiplication

"If you're always wondering how these people post multiple times on multiple platforms at such a high level, it's because of this content multiplication system."

Jay says it's "the thing I've been doing behind the scenes for all the top personal brands in the space" (course 2, ~10:52).

Why start on X (Twitter)

  1. You're not penalized for posting volume — 10+ posts/day is fine.
  2. When a tweet does well, you know the idea (not the visual) is validated — so you can multiply it.

The three R's

Run on any outperforming post:

  1. Repost — republish ~90 days later.

    "The majority of your audience hadn't seen the first post." And for the people who saw both: "we need to be reminded about the core few things more than we need to be taught new things."

  2. Repurpose — take the validated idea and reformat it for each platform as its own channel (Instagram, LinkedIn, ad hook, YouTube video, newsletter).

    "It's not as easy as taking a tweet and posting it on another platform. Treat every platform like a separate channel."

  3. Remix — rewrite the same post in many ways:
    • Same idea, different format: "Winners get better, losers get bitter" → "The fastest way to never get what you want is to be bitter when other people are winning."
    • Different idea, same template: "X people do X, Y people do Y" → "Rich people focus on saving time, poor people focus on saving money."

"You can turn one idea into 10."

The Hall of Fame

"I like to use what I call a hall of fame — take any post that does above your average engagement or impressions and save it to a database: name of the post, link, when it was posted, and whether you've already reposted it."

"By year five or six you're basically just replaying your best hits — like a musician on tour isn't playing new songs, they play the already-proven ones."

Recap

"Step one: build a hall of fame. Step two: use the three R's. If you master generating future demand, the world is yours."

Exercise

  1. Build your Hall of Fame (spreadsheet or doc): 5 of your best posts, with engagement numbers.
  2. Take the top post and produce: 1 repost (90 days out), 2 repurposes (e.g. LinkedIn + Instagram), and 3 remixes.
